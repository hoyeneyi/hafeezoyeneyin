Process

A considered process

Transparent at every stage: what is happening, what comes next and what it costs.

  1. Discovery

    A complimentary 15-minute conversation about your business, your audience and what the site must accomplish.

  2. Proposal & direction

    A fixed-price proposal, followed by two distinct homepage concepts to choose between.

  3. Build & refinement

    Follow progress on a private preview link and refine the details together as it takes shape.

  4. Launch & handover

    Your domain is connected, the site goes live, and you receive a clear guide to managing it.
