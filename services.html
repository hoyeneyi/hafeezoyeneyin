FAQ

Frequently asked

How is a project priced?

Every project is quoted at a fixed price following a complimentary 15-minute consultation, based on what the site needs to accomplish. You'll know the full investment before work begins, and it changes only if the scope does.

Are there ongoing monthly fees?

No. Because your site is custom-built, there's no website-builder subscription. The only recurring cost is your domain name, typically $10–15 per year. Sites with accounts or payments may carry modest usage-based service fees, which are outlined in your proposal.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Sites can include an admin portal for managing programs, events, imagery and announcements, accompanied by a clear guide. On simpler sites, small text changes are handled for you at no charge.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. Your existing content is carried over from your current site or builder, rebuilt to a higher standard, and your domain is moved without disrupting your email. Keep your current plan active until the new site launches.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Clients are welcome from anywhere. Consultations, design reviews and a private live preview all take place online.

What is the typical timeline?

Most sites launch within one to three weeks of the initial consultation. Projects with portals, accounts or payments take longer, and a detailed timeline accompanies every proposal.

How does payment work?

Invoices are issued by Oyeneyin Solutions LLC, with payment details included on each. Any work beyond the agreed scope is quoted separately before it begins.
