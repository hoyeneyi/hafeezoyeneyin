FAQ

Questions

How much does a website cost?

Every project gets a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call, based on what the site needs to do. You'll know the full price before any work starts, and it won't change unless the scope does.

Will I have to pay every month?

No. Your site is custom-coded, so there's no website-builder subscription to keep paying. The only ongoing cost is your domain name, usually about $10–15 a year. Sites with logins or payments may have small usage-based service costs, which the quote spells out.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes, if you need to. Sites can include an admin portal for editing programs, events, photos and announcements, plus a guide that walks you through it. For simpler sites, send me small text changes and I'll make them at no charge.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. I'll bring your content over from your current site or website builder, rebuild it properly and move your domain without breaking your email. Keep your current plan running until the new site is live.

Do you work with clients outside my area?

Yes. Everything runs remotely: calls, design reviews and a live preview link you can open from anywhere.

How long does it take?

Most sites take one to three weeks from the first call to launch. Sites with admin portals, accounts or payments take longer, and you'll get a timeline with the quote.

How does payment work?

Invoices are issued by Oyeneyin Solutions LLC, with payment details on each invoice. Anything outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before I start on it.

Have a project in mind?

Tell me what you're working on. You'll hear back within two business days to set up a free 15-minute call.
